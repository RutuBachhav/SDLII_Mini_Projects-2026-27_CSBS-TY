1.3 Theory: Ensemble Learning is a powerful machine learning technique where multiple models, often called base learners or weak learners, are combined to improve overall performance. The idea is that a group of weak learners can come together to form a strong learner. Ensemble algorithms help to reduce variance, bias, and improve accuracy. The most popular ensemble methods include Bagging, Boosting, and Stacking.
Ensemble Algorithms to Apply:
1.Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating):
oIn bagging, multiple versions of a base learner are trained on different subsets of the dataset (using sampling with replacement).
oCommon algorithm: Random Forest (based on decision trees).
2.Boosting:
oBoosting builds models sequentially, where each model attempts to correct errors made by the previous one.
oCommon algorithms: AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost.
3.Stacking:
oStacking involves training multiple models (first-level learners) and then using another model (meta-learner) to combine the predictions from the first-level models.
oIn stacking, models like decision trees, SVMs, or neural networks are often used as base learners.
