Database tables:
- parking_slots: slot code, vehicle category, availability
- parking_sessions: vehicle, owner, slot, entry time, exit time, fee, status

The JSP pages query MySQL directly for this student project starter. Business operations (check-in/check-out) go through ParkingServlet.
